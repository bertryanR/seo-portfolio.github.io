Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what an SEO specialist actually does, how long results take, and what it costs to get found.

An SEO specialist audits how a site is built, chosen, and linked to, then fixes what's holding it back in search results. That covers keyword research, on-page optimization like titles and headings, technical fixes like site speed and crawlability, content strategy, and building the site's authority through links and mentions.
Most sites see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, with compounding gains after that. Timing depends on how competitive the niche is, how much authority the site already has, and how consistently the work gets done. Anyone promising first-page rankings in a couple of weeks is overselling it.
On-page SEO is everything you control directly on the site: content, headings, internal links, page speed, and structure. Off-page SEO is what happens elsewhere that signals trust back to the site, mainly backlinks, brand mentions, and reviews. Both need to work together for rankings to hold.
