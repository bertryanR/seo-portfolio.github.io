Local SEO Growth for Healthcare services

A Healthcare services company scaled its organic traffic from approximately 600 to 1,900 monthly visitors, achieving over 216% growth through targeted SEO strategies.

OVERVIEW

About the Company: An Australia-based healthcare provider specializing in physiotherapy, exercise physiology, and allied health services. With a growing digital presence, Healthstin aimed to attract more local patients through organic search and convert that traffic into booked appointments across multiple service pages.
Industry: Healthcare
Project Duration:12 Months
Main Goal: Increase organic traffic and boost sales from search.

THE CHALLENGE

The company faced limited organic visibility in a competitive local healthcare market. While the website had multiple service pages, rankings for high-intent keywords like “physio near me” were not fully optimized, resulting in missed traffic and lead opportunities.
Additionally, inconsistent content structure and underutilized SEO strategies led to slow traffic growth and underperformance across key landing pages.

THE SOLUTION

1

Technical SEO & Site Structure Optimization

Conducted a full technical audit to improve crawlability, fix indexing issues, and enhance page speed. I also restructured key service pages to ensure clear internal linking and better alignment with search intent, especially for local queries.

2

Local SEO & Keyword Targeting

I implemented a local SEO strategy focused on high-volume, intent-driven keywords such as “physio near me” (16K monthly searches). This included optimizing on-page elements, creating geo-targeted pages, and improving Google Business signals to dominate local search results.

3

Content Expansion & Page Optimization

I optimized existing service pages and expanded content to better answer user queries and improve engagement. High-performing pages like the “best physio near me” page were enhanced to capture more traffic and convert visitors into leads.

THE RESULTS

  • Total Organic Traffic: Reached 1.9K monthly visitors, showing strong upward growth.
  • Top Page Performance: “Best physio near me” page generated 1,214 visits (+63.8%) alone.
  • Keyword Growth: Individual pages ranking for 25–62 keywords, significantly improving visibility.

THE OUTCOME

Healthstin successfully transformed its organic search presence into a high-performing acquisition channel. By focusing on local SEO, targeted content, and technical improvements, the brand now ranks competitively for high-volume keywords and consistently attracts qualified traffic.
